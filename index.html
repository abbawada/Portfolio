Flagship Project

Betta

The shooting sleeve that learns the shot only you take, then helps you repeat it.

I'm building Betta, a haptic sleeve for basketball. Dual IMUs at the elbow and wrist read the arm 100 times a second and learn your shot signature. When a rep drifts from it, motors answer with directional vibration, steering the next rep back toward the shot your body already knows.

4th of 221

The project placed 4th of 221 teams at USC's 2026 New Venture Seed Competition and won a $10,000 award.

Hand-built

Every circuit built and soldered by hand, from the first breadboard to the protoboard inside the current prototype.

19 for 19

First real on-body capture session, August 4, 2026. Nineteen reps, every one captured and segmented.

Colorway studies for the production sleeve.

App design in progress. Missions, live cues, and a form score for every session.
