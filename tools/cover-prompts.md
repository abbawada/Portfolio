# Game-cover art prompts (paste into ChatGPT, one per project)

The shipped covers (2026-08-16 set) bake the chrome into the art: spine band
with monogram, category and serial; rating box bottom left; ABBA WADA bottom
right; title lettering. The site mounts a vector spine over the left band at
render time (chromeSVG in projects.html), so spine flaws in the art are
hidden, but rating box, author line and title must be generated correctly.
Two covers remain. Attach the project's real photo or footage still so the
art is grounded in it. After generating, check all four corners for
watermarks before sending to me. Never use FLAGSHIP anywhere.

## Master prompt (prepend to each)

Create video-game box cover art, PlayStation-3-era in spirit but with NO
console branding, NO third-party logos, and NO rating-board marks. Portrait
2:3, 1024x1536. Painterly photographic realism, dramatic single-source
lighting, restrained palette built around warm paper (#f7f5f0), ink black
(#141414), and one gold accent (#c6a15b). Left edge: a flat black vertical
spine band, 12% of the width, carrying a small white square monogram reading
"AW" at top, the category word reading top to bottom in white italic caps,
and the serial in small white print at the bottom. Bottom left of the art
area: a white rectangular rating box, black border, a large black letter "S"
over a rule and the word "SIDE QUEST" beneath it. Bottom right: "ABBA WADA"
in small white letterspaced caps. One clear hero subject, centered or
right-of-center, cinematic depth, no clutter, no borders, no watermark.
