# V1.1, Part 5: the mascot, three concepts

Born from Betta and the circuit, in the toy-plastic language of the world,
his the way Milo is Bape's: not a borrowed character, a thing only this
world could have made. Built in-engine behind a review switch
(?mascot=rep, ?mascot=tap, ?mascot=visor) and shown in the three places
it would live: a statue on the lawn beside the court, a figure on the tower head, a
small one on the pit wall by the start flag.
Nothing ships until one is picked; the default page builds none.

| concept | what it is | made of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rep | a basketball that shoots: a gold candy ball with ink seams, dot eyes, stubby graphite legs, and one arm in the smoky sleeve raised in the follow-through, a grey pod on it, a small gold hand | the court and the sleeve |
| Tap | the haptic motor as a creature: a graphite drum body under a gold candy dome, two IMU-module eyes with gold LED pupils, a gold band, an antenna with a cyan tip, boxy feet, grey candy arms | the electronics inside the sleeve |
| Visor | a chrome-visor head on a small gold candy body with a graphite jaw, a gold fin, a cyan scarf, graphite limbs; the mirror head reflects the world | the helmet reference and the race |

Places: the statue (scale 2.6 on a graphite plinth beside the court at
132,108), the tower head (scale 1.8 at the top of the mast), the pit wall
(scale 0.75 beside the board).

Renders: part-5/concept-rep.png, concept-tap.png, concept-visor.png (each
a row of the three places), plus the nine single shots.

Decision (Abba: "you decide", 2026-09-08): Rep. It is the one only this
world could have made, a basketball that shoots with the sleeve on, and it
reads at every scale. Rep ships at the three places; Tap and Visor are
removed from the code. The review switch is gone.
