# V1.1, Part 6 proposal: collectibles and liveliness (for approval before building)

## Case spines, era-styled
The cases keep their art, chrome, tilt, gloss, flip and spread. The spine
evolves toward an era band, the way ref-game-case-collectible reads at a
glance: a coloured district stripe across the top of the spine (gold for
Wearables, green for Token Economics, cool grey for Robotics and IoT, red
stays on Bluetooth Car's Greatest Hits), the AW monogram and the category
wordmark as they are, and a small edition tag at the foot above the
serial: WADA RING. Live SVG in chromeSVG, no cover art regenerated.
Side-by-side of one case before and after comes first; projects.html and
the panels share cases.js so both change together.
